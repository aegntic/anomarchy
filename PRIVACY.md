# Privacy Policy for Omarchy Mobile (`anomarchy`)

**Effective Date:** September 13, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Developer:** @aegntic (aegntic.dev@gmail.com)  
**Pedigree:** Inspired by David Heinemeier Hansson (DHH) Omakase philosophy.

---

### Summary: 100% Private, 0 Data Collected

Omarchy Mobile is built on developer sovereignty and strict privacy. **We do not collect, track, sell, or transmit any personal data, analytics, or behavioral telemetry to any third party or remote server.** All files, terminal history, scripts, and configurations remain strictly on your own device and/or your direct self-hosted workstation.

---

### 1. Information Collection & Use
- **No Accounts Required:** You do not need to register, sign in, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to use any feature of the application.
- **No Analytics / No Trackers:** There are zero third-party telemetry SDKs, trackers, advertising identifiers, or analytics libraries embedded in the application.
- **Local Virtual Filesystem:** All storage (`/home/omarchy`, `/projects`, `/scripts`) is maintained strictly on your device's sandboxed storage.

---

### 2. Device Permissions
The application requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for local developer tooling:
- **Internet / Network Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):** Used exclusively to establish peer connections to your self-hosted workstation or remote Linux machine when configured. No data passes through intermediate servers.
- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`, Optional):** Used strictly for on-device scanning of QR codes generated by the terminal pairing bridge. Video frames are processed in real-time in memory and are never recorded, saved, or transmitted.
- **Vibration (`android.permission.VIBRATE`):** Used to provide tactile haptic feedback during interactive terminal typing and touch gestures.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising
Omarchy Mobile is completely ad-free. No advertising networks, data brokers, or profiling partners are integrated.

---

### 4. Data Deletion
Since Omarchy Mobile collects zero user data, no remote personal data is stored. Uninstalling the application or clearing its app storage instantly and irreversibly removes all local sandbox files and caches from your device.
